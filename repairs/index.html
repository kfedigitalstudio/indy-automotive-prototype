See how the service process works

The slideshow and service steps move together so customers can see what is happening at each stage of the repair process.

Step 1 • Inspect

Initial vehicle inspection

A technician reviews the vehicle, checks the reported concern and looks for visible wear or related issues.

Step 2 • Diagnose

Pinpoint the cause

Testing and hands-on diagnosis help identify what is actually causing the symptom before repairs are recommended.

Step 3 • Repair

Perform the approved work

Once the customer approves the repair, the technician completes the service using the appropriate tools and replacement parts.

Step 4 • Final Check

Verify the repair

The repaired system is checked again so the shop can confirm the work addressed the concern before the vehicle is returned.
