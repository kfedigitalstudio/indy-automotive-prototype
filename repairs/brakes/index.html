Your brakes are one of the most important safety systems on your vehicle. This dedicated service page shows how Indy Auto Repairs can explain common symptoms, inspections, repairs, and next steps without making customers hunt for information.

What we check

A complete brake inspection can include pad thickness, rotor condition, calipers, hoses, fluid, pedal feel, warning lights and a road-test assessment when appropriate.

Brake pad and shoe wear
Rotor and drum condition
Calipers, hoses and hardware
Brake fluid level and condition
Pedal feel and braking response

Signs your brakes may need attention

Squealing, scraping or grinding noises
Longer stopping distance
Vibration when braking
Brake pedal feels soft or unusually firm
Vehicle pulls while braking
Brake warning light stays on

Why timely brake service matters

Brake problems can affect stopping distance, vehicle control and overall safety. Catching worn components early may also help prevent damage to related parts and reduce the cost of a larger repair later.
