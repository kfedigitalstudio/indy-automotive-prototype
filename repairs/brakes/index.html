Signs your brakes may need attention

Brake components wear gradually, and early warning signs can help you address a problem before it becomes more serious. If you notice any of the symptoms below, schedule an inspection.

Squealing or grinding sounds
Vehicle pulling while braking
Vibration in the pedal or steering wheel
Soft or unusually firm brake pedal
Longer stopping distance
Brake or ABS warning light

What we inspect

A brake service visit can include inspection of pads, rotors, calipers, brake fluid, hoses, hardware and related components. The final recommendation depends on wear, condition and the vehicle manufacturer’s specifications.

Brake service FAQs

How often should brakes be inspected?Inspection frequency depends on driving habits, mileage, vehicle type and manufacturer recommendations. An inspection is also appropriate whenever symptoms appear.
Do squealing brakes always mean I need new pads?Not always. Noise can have several causes, so an inspection is the best way to determine what is happening.
Can I schedule brake service online?Yes. Use the service request form above and the shop can confirm availability.
